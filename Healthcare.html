About Us

Pioneering Home Healthcare in Nepal

Health Home Care Nepal was established with a singular vision: to provide accessible, high-quality, and compassionate medical care right in the comfort of your home. We understand the challenges families face when managing the health of their loved ones.

Our team comprises fully certified nurses, skilled physiotherapists, and experienced doctors who are dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for our patients across the Kathmandu valley and beyond.
